The goal of Boulesteix_replic is to provide an easily reproducible format for the analysis and simulation performed by Boulesteix, Groenwold, Abrahamowicz, et al. (2020).

Author: Logan Groenestein
Based on contents provided in the Markup Languages and Reproducible Programming in Statistics course and the paper by  Boulesteix, Groenwold, Abrahamowicz, et al. (2020).
